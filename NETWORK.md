**Live:** https://huepot.net  
**Read with:** `GROWTH.md` (what not to do) and `UPDATE.md` (product).  
**Live product:** v1.3.26 is on https://huepot.net (invite, cashier, Classic hour, Fog cup). Open Account for your code before you ask strangers to share.

Huepot does not grow from a logo. It grows from a **small network of people who sit the same table, get paid, and send one link**. Empty Classic kills every other tactic.

This is the operating plan. Do it in order. Do not skip to influencers.

---

## What “network” means here

Not a new chain. Not Discord for its own sake. Not a follower count.

A Huepot network is:

1. **A live pit** — Classic is almost never empty when someone lands.
2. **A proof trail** — strangers can see a take, a fairness seed, and at least one paid cash-out.
3. **A hangout** — one Telegram (or one X account) where the same people hear “sitting Classic now.”
4. **An invite graph** — your rake-invite link, then theirs. Money for sharing comes from **house rake only**.

Twenty people who deposit twice beat two thousand Google clicks that bounce off a dead room.

## Week 1 — Seed the network (people you already have)

Do not hunt strangers yet. Hunt **trust**.

**Who:** 5–15 people who already know you (crypto friends, Telegram contacts, one small group chat). 18+ only. Tell them it is real money and they can lose it.

**What you do each day (30–60 min):**

1. Sit Classic (and Fog if you have 20 extra minutes). Small clicks. Keep the pot from looking dead.
2. Pay any queued withdrawal the same day.
3. Post **one** proof in the hangout you pick (below): a take, a fairness page, or a paid BscScan. No private keys. No fake bank.
4. Send **your** invite link to 3 people, not 30. Ask them to sit one 1 USDT click on Classic while you are there.

**Hangout (pick one, ignore the rest for 30 days):**

- **Telegram** is the default. Name it something plain (`Huepot pit`). Pin: rule in one sentence, Classic link, your invite link, “cash-out is same day.”
- X / crypto Twitter only if you already live there. Do not open both and ghost both.

**What you do not do this week:** directories, influencers, spam in BNB groups, “who wants free USDT,” fake seats.

**Week 1 done when:** at least **3 people who are not you** have signed in, **2 have deposited**, and **1 has withdrawn** (or clearly chosen to keep playing after a take). If that fails, stay in Week 1. The product is not the bottleneck; the empty room is.

## Week 2–3 — One public table, one public hour

Now the network has a **time**, not only a URL.

1. **Name the session.** Example: “Classic open — 20:00 UTC, 1 USDT clicks.” Post it in Telegram the same morning. Sit it yourself.
2. **Fog as the clip, Classic as the meet.** Record the last 12 seconds of Fog (board goes dark, then the take). 8–15 seconds. Post that. Caption ends with Classic + invite link. Fog is the hook; Classic is where a stranger can sit without a host.
3. **Custom Fog table after a take.** When someone wins, you (or they) open a 30-minute Fog table and send **that** link to 3 people. Custom rooms are the viral object. House rooms stay the storefront.
4. **Reply, don’t raid.** In BNB / USDT / “color game” threads, one sentence + link. If a mod hates gambling talk, leave. No copy-paste raids.

**Invite graph (this is the product loop):**

- You send `https://huepot.net/signin?ref=YOURCODE`.
- They sit. After a take that creates rake, you see **Invite** on Account.
- You tell them: copy **their** code and send it to two people. Their cut is also rake-only, 10 USDT/day cap.

Do not promise “deposit 10 get 5.” You cannot afford that.

**Week 2–3 done when:** Classic has been non-empty for a **named hour** three days in a row without you begging in DMs, **or** one person who is not you has opened a custom table.

## Week 4 — Only if payouts are boring

If cash-outs are still slow, stop. Fix the queue.

If cash-outs are same-day and Classic still dies when you stand up:

- Stay in the same hangout. Increase **session reliability**, not channel count.
- Ask one regular to host Tuesday. You host Thursday. Two faces beat one louder account.

If Classic stays busy **without** you for a few sessions:

- One English-speaking BNB micro-influencer, paid as a **posted table buy-in you can afford** (you sit with them), not a $500 tweet.
- One listing on a free play-to-earn / on-chain directory. One. Read their rules. No fake TVL.

Fog cup is already named (Sunday 21:00 UTC). Use it after Classic hour already has people, not instead of Classic.

**Do not say:** guaranteed win, “provably fair casino” hype, other brands’ names, odds, sportsbook, “risk-free,” fake volume.

## What not to build for this plan

- More house rooms, FX, leaderboards, achievements.
- Multi-chain deposits.
- A marketing site, blog, or second domain.
- Discord + Telegram + X + TikTok at once.
- More sit windows, RSVP, or a second hangout before Classic hour already works.

The invite loop is already live. The missing piece is **people in the pit at the same time**.
